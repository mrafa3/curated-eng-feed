# What counts as relevant

This file tells the curator what to pick. Edit it freely; the next run reads the new version.

## Who this is for

An analytics engineering practitioner who works on curated data models in a lakehouse, experimentation and evidence-based decision-making, production forecasting, and applying LLM agents to data work such as documentation and operational triage. The reader is a practitioner leading a working team, not a researcher: pick posts with ideas that transfer to that setting.

## Pick posts about

- **Experimentation practice:** A/B test design and analysis, variance reduction, power, outliers, sequential testing, causal inference when randomization is imperfect, and how organizations raise experiment quality and culture.
- **Forecasting:** Demand and time-series forecasting in production, hierarchical or sparse data, handling shocks and drift, when to retrain or rebuild.
- **Analytics engineering and the gold layer:** Data modeling, metric and semantic layers, metric governance and ownership, data quality, data contracts, lakehouse and open table format patterns that affect how curated tables get built and trusted.
- **AI agents for data work:** Text-to-SQL, analytics assistants, agent evaluation, LLM-as-judge, agent observability and tracing, harness design.
- **Organizational dynamics and leading data teams:** How data, analytics, and engineering teams are structured, staffed, and split into ownership boundaries; how they set priorities and resolve competing demands; how they adopt AI and change how work gets done; engineering culture and ways of working; what changed after a reorg or a shift in team process, and what the people running it learned. Retrospectives with a candid account of what went wrong organizationally are especially welcome. This does not override the skip list: a post about mobile, frontend, recommender architecture, or low-level infrastructure stays skipped even when it draws an organizational lesson from the work.

## Skip posts about

- Mobile, frontend, and app platform work
- Recommender and ad-ranking model architecture (unless the lesson is about evaluation or experimentation)
- Low-level infrastructure: Kubernetes, JVM, networking, GPU serving, security plumbing
- Hiring announcements, event promos, and company news. A post about how a team is organized or staffed is in scope; a post advertising open roles is not.

## The bar

Pick a post only if it has an idea the reader could apply or adapt, not just an interesting system at a company far larger than theirs. Two strong picks beat six middling ones. An empty week is fine.

## Writing the note

One or two plain sentences on what's useful and how it connects to the reader's work. No hype, no "this post explores." Say the specific idea worth taking.
